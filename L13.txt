For this assesment, I chose the WHO(World Health Organization Website) and i am focusing on the "percievable" criterion.

- Issue 1 : The charts on the website dont have an alt text that is descriptive enough.
Recommendation 1: For complex sharts and graphs, provide a short text summary and include a detailed Description in the body.

- Issue 2 : Some text overlayed on images are not very visisble
Recommendation 1: Use a different contrast(darker color) as background color for text, to ensure better readability.
Recommendation 2: Ensure all standard body text meets or exceeds the 4.5:1 contrast ratio required for Level AA.

- Issue 3: Asynchronized closed captions on videos
Recommendation 1: Provide high quality, human reviewed captions for all video content
Recommendation 2: Provide audio descriptions for pre-recorded videos that contain important visual-only information (e.g., text displayed on screen that isn't spoken).
